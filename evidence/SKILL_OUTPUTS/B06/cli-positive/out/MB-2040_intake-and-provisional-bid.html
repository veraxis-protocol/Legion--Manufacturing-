Subject: RE: RFQ - motor bracket MB-2040 rev C — a few questions before we quote

Hi Dana,

Thanks for sending MB-2040 rev C. We'd like to quote it and have a few questions so the
price we send is firm and not hedged.

1. Drawing. We received the rev C specification sheet (dated 27 Oct) but no dimensioned
   drawing or 3D model. Could you send the rev C drawing (PDF) and a STEP file if you have one?
   We need the bore and slot locations, which way the slot runs, the datum scheme, corner
   radii and the M5 thread class and depth. Without a drawing we can only give you a
   budgetary figure.

2. Flatness. Raj mentioned on 29 Oct that your design team is still deciding the flatness of
   the mounting face. Do you have a value or a date for it yet, and which face is the mounting
   face? If it comes with a new revision (rev D), please tell us so we quote the right one.
   Otherwise we can quote rev C with flatness excluded and add it later.

3. Anodize and the bore. Type II anodize adds a coating that uses up much of the 22 mm H7
   tolerance band. Do the H7 size and the Ra 1.6 finish apply after anodize? Should the bore
   and the M5 threads be masked? Can you also confirm Class 2 (dyed black) and any coating
   thickness requirement?

4. Marking. Where on the part should the part number and lot code go, in what lot-code format,
   and at what character height?

5. Quantities and schedule. Are 250, 500 and 1,000 total order quantities? After first articles
   on 15 Jan 2027, how many pieces per monthly delivery? How many pieces do you want for first
   article, and do they count toward the order? Is 15 Jan a ship date or a date at your dock?

6. Quality requirements. You asked for an AS9102-format FAI. Does the order carry an AS9100 or
   other quality-system requirement, or any other flow-downs? For the material cert, is a mill
   certificate to ASTM B209 enough, or do you need EN 10204 3.1?

7. Commercial. When do you need our quote? Please also send your payment terms, freight terms
   and ship-to address, any packaging requirement for the anodized parts, and your purchase
   terms and conditions if they apply.

If 1 and 2 will take a while, answers to 3 to 7 would still let us move ahead.
