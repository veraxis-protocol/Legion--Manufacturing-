If volumes rise well beyond 1,000, the die-cast economics (die life 100k shots) should be re-run; no volume beyond 1,000 was supplied, so that crossover is not computed here.

  • Tooling scored as pass/fail. A's full tooling score rests on the matrix entry of 0, whose source is not stated (fixturing or programming cost may exist). It does not change the winner (see sensitivity).
