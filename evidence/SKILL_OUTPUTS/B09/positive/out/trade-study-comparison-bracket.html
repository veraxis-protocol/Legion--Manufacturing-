Sensitivity

Stable, with one named flip. A - 6061 machined wins at the stated weights by 0.073 over B - 7075 machined and 0.156 over C - A380 die cast. It wins 368 of 381 weightings when all five weights move jointly by up to ±10 points, and 19868 of 20000 random weightings (each weight scaled 0.5-1.5×).

The decision rests on the unit-cost weight. Raising unit_cost_at_1000 from the stated 30% to 47.6% (others rescaled in proportion) hands the win to C - A380 die cast; if tooling_cost is also dropped to 0%, the flip comes earlier, at 41.2% unit cost. All 13 grid weightings that C - A380 die cast wins have unit cost at 0.35-0.40 and lead time at 0.10-0.15: a cost-dominated regime that also discounts first-article lead time.

Stiffness would have to reach 87.8% before B - 7075 machined wins. Tooling, mass and lead time produce no flip at any weight from 0 to 100%.

B - 7075 machined never wins under any plausible weighting (0 of 381 grid, 0 of 20000 random); it is not a close alternative.

Normalization caveat on tooling_cost. A and B are entered at 0, so ratio normalization (best ÷ value) degenerates: A and B score 1.000 and C scores 0.000, a pass/fail rather than a proportion. With tooling weight set to 0 the winner is unchanged (A - 6061 machined 0.840, C - A380 die cast 0.778, B - 7075 machined 0.759), but C moves ahead of B. The recommendation does not rest on this choice; the B/C order does.

Noise. The supplied files state no precision for any score, so whether the 0.073 A-B gap lies outside input noise cannot be tested formally. The closest inputs are on stiffness, A 412 Hz against B 418 Hz, a difference the recommendation does not rely on.
