D2 — Problem description

At final inspection on 2026-11-11, Bore A on sampled MB-1880 rev B parts from lot L-0412 measured 22.029 to 22.034 mm on 3 of 5 sampled parts, against your drawing MB-1880 rev B, sheet 2, note 4: dia 22.000 +0.021/-0.000 mm (max 22.021). The excess was 0.008 to 0.013 mm. Our in-process records show the first part of the lot was already at 22.022.

Affected quantity: at least 300 — all of lot L-0412. The preceding lot of this part, L-0411, is not yet ruled out and is being evaluated.

This is a repeat occurrence. The same condition (bore oversize, 22.026 mm) occurred on MB-1880 in April 2026. The corrective action then (a fixed insert-change interval) was not verified as effective, and the condition has recurred.

The boring insert was replaced on 2026-11-11; the first part after replacement measured 22.007 mm. All held stock will be 100% measured on Bore A before any disposition.

D4 — Root cause (under verification)

The root cause is not yet confirmed. Our measurements show Bore A growing steadily across four consecutive lots made with one boring insert (10 consecutive increasing readings), and returning to 22.007 mm after the insert was replaced. Two causes fit this equally well and are being separated by test:

  1. Progressive change of the boring insert cutting edge over its life, because the insert-change interval was not controlling insert life.
  2. Drift of the boring-head diameter setting or tool offset during the run, reset when the insert was changed.

A coolant concentration change made before these lots, and the measuring gage, are being checked and are currently less likely. Verification: examination of the removed insert and review of the machine's tool-offset history.

D5 — Corrective actions (proposed)

  • Insert life enforced by a machine part counter, at an interval set from measured bore growth.
  • In-process Bore A checks with a reaction limit inside the tolerance and a trend rule, so action is taken before the limit is reached.
  • Boring-head setting verified and locked at every insert change; all tool-offset changes logged.

The final choice follows the D4 verification.

  • Tool-life limits for critical bores set from measured data and enforced in the machine control.
  • Statistical process control with trend rules on critical diameters, starting with MB-1880 Bore A.
