Systemic prevention (D7-type) — why CAPA-071 did not hold

  • CAPA-071 was never verified effective (prior_ncrs.csv: capa_effective = “not verified”) and the same defect returned 211 days later. Either the cause in NCR-0219 was wrong or the 600-part change was not actually carried out. The system change: a CAPA cannot close without a dated effectiveness check against the characteristic that failed (here, Bore A capability over subsequent MB-1880 lots), and an open-but-unverified CAPA is reviewed at a fixed interval.
  • Tool-life control by counter, not by memory: insert intervals set by a CAPA are implemented as machine/tool-crib limits that are auditable (the inputs hold no part count for T7-0031 at all — that absence is itself the gap).
  • Read the NCR-0219 investigation before closing this one: its records (not in the inputs) should show whether insert wear was demonstrated or assumed.
  • Clock discipline: inspection, shipping and maintenance records on one stated time zone, so a hold, a shipment and a repair can be ordered without dispute (this investigation could not order them).
