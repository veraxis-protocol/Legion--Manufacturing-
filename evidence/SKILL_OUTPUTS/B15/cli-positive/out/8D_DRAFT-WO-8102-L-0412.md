## D4 — Root cause (ranked candidates, uncertainty kept)

**Confirmed (shared trunk):**
- **T1.** Insert T7-0031 cut parts from 2026-11-09 07:10 to 2026-11-10 21:50 across **4 lots** with **no change recorded**. Evidence: `tool_change_since_last = no` on all 9 later T7-0031 rows. Maintenance shows no T7 event until 2026-11-11 06:40.
- **T2.** Bore A rose **26.0 µm** over that span. The longest increasing run is **10 points** and R² vs time is **0.876**. After the insert change, L-0413 #1 measured **22.007**, a step of **−27.0 µm**.
- *Against T2:* only one post-change point exists.

**Ranking**

| Rank | Candidate | Weak point |
|---|---|---|
| **1 (tie)** | **C1: insert-edge degradation** (wear, chipping or BUE on 6061) grew the cut diameter | "Insert count exceeded the 600-part limit" has no verified For evidence. The only computed bound is ≥390 parts. Also, plain flank wear normally makes a bore *smaller* |
| **1 (tie)** | **C2: T7 offset or boring-head setting drifted outward**, and the insert change reset it | "Offset incremented / head crept" has no record in the data |
| 3 | C3: coolant 6 %→8 % (2026-11-08 16:00) raised the wear rate | No baseline (0 inspection rows before the change). Size reset with no coolant reversal |
| 4 | C4: shift, operator or thermal effect | Shift and operator are fully confounded. Drift ran through shift boundaries (+1.0, +1.0, +3.0 µm) with no overnight reset |
| 5 | C5: measurement error | In-process and final inspection agree |

**C1. Confirm:** retained T7-0031 shows BUE, chipping or a changed nose geometry, **or** T7-0031 refitted at the current offset cuts about 22.03.
**C1. Kill:** T7-0031 shows only flank wear, **or** refitted it cuts near nominal.
**C2. Confirm:** the VMC-03 T7 offset history shows outward wear-offset entries during 11-09 to 11-11, or the head setting changed at the insert change.
**C2. Kill:** the offset history shows no change from 11-09 07:10 to 11-11 06:40, and the head was untouched.
**C3. Confirm:** the growth rate on T7-0032 at 8 % clearly exceeds the historical rate at 6 %.
**C3. Kill:** the rates match.
**C4. Confirm:** an overnight drop or within-shift rise appears on T7-0032.
**C4. Kill:** a run on T7-0032 across a shift change shows no step.
**C5. Confirm:** a gauge GR&R or calibration check fails.
**C5. Kill:** 5 quarantined parts re-measured on a second calibrated gauge or CMM agree within gauge uncertainty.

**Cheapest discriminating test:** pull the VMC-03 T7 offset history for 2026-11-09 07:00 to 2026-11-11 07:05 and examine T7-0031 under magnification. This separates **C1 from C2**. In the same pull, take the WO-8099/8100/8101 completed quantities. They settle C1's weak link and show whether CAPA-071 was **not followed** or **set wrong**. *Not yet run, so D4 stays tied.*

**Escape point:** the SPC trend rule (6 increasing) fired at **2026-11-10 07:20** (L-0411 #1), **7.83 h** before the first out-of-spec part. No in-process reaction plan existed.
**System cause:** CAPA-071's 600-part limit had no enforcing mechanism, and its effectiveness was never verified.
