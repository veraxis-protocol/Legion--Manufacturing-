# VMC spindle troubleshooting (excerpt, Section 7.4)
| Symptom | Possible cause | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Overtemp with rising vibration | Spindle bearing wear / preload loss | Vibration spectrum at bearing frequencies; run-out at taper (< 3 um) |
| Overtemp, normal vibration | Chiller low flow / fluid level | Chiller flow and level |
| Overload with tool pull-out marks | Drawbar clamp force low | Clamp force gauge (spec 8.0-9.5 kN) |
| Grinding noise | Bearing damage or contamination | Stop machine; do not run spindle; manual rotation feel |
SAFETY: Lock out the spindle drive before any manual rotation or inspection. Do not run a spindle showing grinding noise.
